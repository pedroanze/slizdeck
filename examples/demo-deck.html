Why this exists

The problem with deck tools

Slide editors

Slow

→ Hours of dragging boxes

Every deck starts from a blank canvas. Alignment, spacing and styling eat the time you should spend on the story.

Templates

Rigid

→ Same deck every time

Fixed layouts force your content into someone else's structure. The narrative bends to the template.

AI exporters

Opaque

→ No control over output

One-shot generation with no wireframe step. You get a file you cannot easily inspect, diff or edit.

Three moves

From brief to deck

01

Brief

Topic, audience, type, duration. One quick round of questions, then the agent proposes a narrative arc.

02

Wireframe

One line per slide, ASCII layout for the complex ones. You approve the structure before any code is written.

03

Generate

The agent assembles the deck from the pattern catalog, opens it in your browser and iterates with you.
